# Farmer Daily — public requirements summary

## Purpose

Help Washington growers understand what matters today using farm context, public agricultural science and source-grounded explanations. Prioritize useful, accessible and affordable support without presenting uncertain observations as established agronomic diagnoses.

## Core workflow

Farmer enters fields, crops, dates, tasks and preferences → permitted tools retrieve outside data → bounded rules identify relevant changes → optional AI selects read-only tools and explains evidence → farmer reviews a short brief, sources and possible next steps.

## Product capabilities

- Farm/field profiles and a visual boundary editor; field identity links context, history, tasks and advisories.
- Daily three-priority brief, field weather, official alerts and elevation.
- Additional farm-context adapters and dated satellite measurements, with source-specific consent, freshness and availability labels.
- Optional model routing: lower-cost lightweight summaries and stronger personalization/comparisons, with actual returned model labels and cost estimates rather than promised savings.
- Themes, reading support and device-voice playback.
- Private records, administrator access requests and approved workflows; integrations must disclose their provider setup and verification requirements.

## Safety and privacy

Per-owner data isolation; trusted server identity; no client-supplied administrator privilege; no private keys in client assets; minimized approved AI evidence; spending reservations; explicit approvals for external actions; recipient verification and opt-out for messaging. Empty public-copy administrator configuration grants no administrator access.

## Vision versus implementation

Government-funded morning briefs and optional referrals to specialist agricultural AI businesses are a policy/business proposal. Satellite indicators do not establish disease. Current source access does not imply NOAA, NASA, USDA or university endorsement. License-dependent integrations must be completed under permitted terms before being represented as working.
