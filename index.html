    YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

    Privacy & security

    See what is stored, where it goes, and remove it whenever you choose.

    ◇
    Private local prototype

    Your farm profile, phone, address, tasks, observations, and job history stay in this browser. Only the coordinates of the community you selected are sent through our server to NOAA/NWS or Open-Meteo for a forecast—never your name, address, phone, field names, crops, or notes.

    Data minimization

    Address and phone are optional. Messaging channels remain off until separately enabled.

    No model training

    Farmer Daily must never use private farm data to train shared models without separate, informed permission.

    Approval boundaries

    External messages, purchases, submissions, and irreversible changes always require explicit approval.

    Production security

    Encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, least-privilege access, audit logs, backups, and incident response are launch requirements.

    Your local data

    Profile, tasks, observations, and agent history. Account access and request history are retained.
